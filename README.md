# Claude

## Projects

- [**Pokopia Companion Guide**](pokopia-companion/): an unofficial companion app for Pokémon Pokopia on Nintendo Switch 2, for Android, the web, and the ESP32 "Cheap Yellow Display" touchscreen board. It tracks your Pokédex (with pictures of all 1,025 Pokémon), habitats, Ditto's moves, story areas, meals and requests. See its README for how to install the APK.
- [**Multiview Browser**](multiview-browser/): a private Android browser for watching videos. Videos keep playing in the background, up to four tabs play side by side, ads and trackers are blocked, and saved videos are rebuilt into clean, encrypted files. It also has tab groups, casts to a TV, and keeps sessions and bookmarks with moving video previews.
